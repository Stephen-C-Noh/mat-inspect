MAT‑Inspect

Every crane, forklift, and truck gets checked before anyone uses it. On a phone, before the shift starts.

The old way

A paper sheet in a binder proves nothing

A missing page looks exactly like a page nobody filled in.

The new way
ScanPoint the phone at the tag on the machine
CheckWork the list, item by item
DoneConfirmed, timestamped, filed instantly

The checklist that opens is the right one for that exact machine.

When something is wrong

The machine stops. Right then.

Out of service

A failed check locks the equipment out and tells a supervisor. Nobody has to notice a note.

Gloves on, hands dirty

Just say what is broken

Speak the defect and it becomes text. The audio never leaves our own machines.

Why it holds up

Once written, it cannot be changed

Every inspection is sealed in a tamper-evident log. Not by policy. By the way it is built.
