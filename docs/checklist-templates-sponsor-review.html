MAT-Inspect — Checklist Templates for Sponsor Review

Prepared by the MAT-Inspect capstone team for sponsor approval (DEV-14).
Please confirm that the items below match the paper checklists used by operators, then reply with approval so this can be recorded in the project ticket.

Severity key:
Blocking — equipment must not be operated until the defect is repaired (red status in the app).
Warning — defect is noted and reported to the supervisor, but equipment may continue to operate (yellow status in the app).

#ItemCriteriaSeverity
